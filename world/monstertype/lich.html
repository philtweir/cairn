Monster Type

Lich

Powerful sorcerers that paid the ultimate price for immortality: their own life. Now their thirst for power knows no bounds. Maintain unlife by binding themselves to an earthly artifact, which is typically protected by at least d6 other undead servants. Carry up to six of the following spellbooks: Animate Object, Arcane Eye, Deafen, Disassemble, Elemental Wall, Frenzy, Gate, Hover, Hatred, Identify Owner, Knock, Pacify, Raise Dead, Raise Spirit, Shield, Sleep, Smoke Form, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Teleport, Vision, Wizard Mark.
