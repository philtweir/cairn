Monster Type

White Dragon

A massive winged creature of frost that lairs comfortably in only the coldest regions. Perpetually accompanied by a storm of ice and wind, the White Dragon is a manifestation of winter itself. Frost Breath: Nearby targets must make a DEX save or are frozen solid, their breath turned to icicles in their lungs.
