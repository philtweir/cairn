Monster Type

Zombie

Re-animated corpse brought back through necromantic ritual or fell influence. Immune to all mind-influencing effects. If not utterly destroyed (by fire, complete dismemberment, etc.), the Zombie will rise again within a few minutes. Critical Damage: Target is infected and becomes deprived. Without the services of a specialist or healer, they will become a zombie in d4 days.
