Monster Type

Roc

Gargantuan birds of prey that roost in high plateau and distant mountain peaks. Powerful hunters, they will defend a clutch of eggs to the death. Grab: Their powerful talons can scoop up entire groups of people (targets must all make a DEX save to escape). Victims are carried back to the nest to be eaten alive. Critical Damage: Target is pierced through their middle, dying instantly.
