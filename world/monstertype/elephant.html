Monster Type

Elephant

Large, intelligent animals that dwell in deserts and plains, usually in herds. Their tusks are worth 1000gp to the right buyer. There are some who consider taking the tusks as immoral. Charge: Nearby targets must make a DEX save or be trampled underfoot (d12 STR damage, ignoring armor).
