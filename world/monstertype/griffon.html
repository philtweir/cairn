Monster Type

Griffon

Ravenous hybrids, with the head and wings of an eagle and the lower body of a lion. Can be used as mounts if taken young and properly trained. Fly and attack at astonishing speeds, diving in to scoop up their prey. Critical Damage: Tears flesh and bone from its victim with its razor-sharp beak.
