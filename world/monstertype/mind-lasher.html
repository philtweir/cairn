Monster Type

Mind Lasher

Humanoid creatures with purple skin and squid-like heads. Dwell deep underground or in spacefaring vessels. Feed on the brains of other humanoids, absorbing their memories and life essence. Highly intelligent, but evil beyond belief. Mind Blast: Nearby targets must save WIL or lose 1d4 WIL and become paralyzed. Critical Damage: A tentacle rips the victim's brain out of their skull.
