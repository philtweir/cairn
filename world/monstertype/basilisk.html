Monster Type

Basilisk

Long, serpentine lizards that nest deep below the earth or in brambles just underfoot. Fighting a Basilisk without meeting its gaze is difficult. (Attacks facing it are impaired.) Gaze: A target facing the Basilisk is slowly petrified from the bottom up and completely frozen after three rounds. An antidote can be brewed from the Basilisk's gizzards. The creature's reflection is harmless.
