Monster Type

Wyvern

Large, intelligent, winged reptiles. Dwell in any terrain, but favor dry cliffs. Shy away from other creatures, preferring to silently stalk their targets from afar. Critical Damage: The victim is impaled and held in place. The wyvern then bites their head off.
