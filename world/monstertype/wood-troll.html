Monster Type

Wood Troll

Large humanoids with elongated arms made of bark or root. They live in forests, sharing a deep connection with the vegetation around them. If killed, they will slowly regenerate unless their corpse is taken out of the forest or burned with fire. Critical Damage: Moss and twigs begin growing out of the target's wounds.
