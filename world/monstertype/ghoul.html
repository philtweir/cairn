Monster Type

Ghoul

Grey-skinned man-things wearing shredded clothes that crave only the flesh of the living. Critical Damage: Target is deprived from their necrotic wound, dying in d4 days unless treated by a priest or skilled healer. If the body is not burned it later rises as a Ghoul as well.
