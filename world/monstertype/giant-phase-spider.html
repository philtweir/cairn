Monster Type

Giant Phase Spider

8’ long, black spiders that have the ability to become intangible. Dwell in web-filled lairs and sometimes prey on humans. Can freely shift in and out of its current plane, phasing back to attack. It cannot phase out again until its next turn. Critical Damage: Without specialist healing the target begins to slowly phase out of reality over 1d6 days.
