Monster Type

Harpy

Hideous monstrosities with the body of a giant eagle and a humanoid head. Their movements and flight are clumsy due to their disproportionate size. Rely on charmed victims to do anything of note. Song: Anyone nearby must save WIL or be charmed by its voice, following the harpy and defending it from any threat.
