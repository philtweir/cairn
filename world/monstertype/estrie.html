Monster Type

Estrie

Appears as a woman with long dark hair that extends all the way to her feet. Can transform into owls, but only at night. Binding their hair prevents transformation. Drinks the blood of small animals and sleeping travelers, who dream of feathers and the moon. If killed they resurrect at the next full moon. This can be stopped by burying the head under ground and filling the mouth with sand. Critical Damage: target falls unconscious for 1d6 hours.
