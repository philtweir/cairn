Giant Mantis

Cow-sized insects with sharp forelimbs and slicing mandibles. Dwell in tropical forests and jungles. Relies on its dark green coloration to camouflage in foliage and attack unsuspecting prey. Critical Damage: Target loses a body part. Roll 1d6. 1: Head, 2-4: Leg, 5-6: Arm.
