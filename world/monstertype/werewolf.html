Monster Type

Werewolf

Ferocious lycanthropes that can transform into a wolf-shape at will. Mundane attacks are impaired against werewolves, but attacks with weapons made of silver are enhanced. Critical Damage: Target becomes infected with lycanthropy, transforming into a werewolf by the next full moon.
