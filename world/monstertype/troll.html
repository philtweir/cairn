Monster Type

Troll

Giant, warty humanoids of flesh and bark. Dwell underground or in deep forests, as well as the ruined homes of their former victims. Carnivorous. When taking Critical Damage, a Troll will continue fighting, despite loss of flesh and limb. Only when their STR is 0 are they truly killed. Unless their heads are bathed in acid, fire, or powerful magic, they will fully regenerate within 1d4 days.
