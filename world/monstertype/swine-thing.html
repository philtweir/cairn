Swine Thing

Obese humanoids that transform into massive porcine monstrosities to feast on human flesh. Prefer to hunt in isolated settlements close to forests and wetlands. Can only shapeshift at night. Charm: Target obeys the swine's every command until it is killed. WIL save once per round to break free.
