Monster Type

Boggart

Wild, hairy tricksters that prize shiny trinkets above coin. Boggarts have true names that describe their true nature. Knowing these names gives a person control over them. Magic: The Boggart can bring objects to life, sow confusion, create magical disguises, use telekinesis, and teleport.
