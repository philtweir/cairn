Red Cap

Born from blood left to rot in the land of Fairy. Their hats are a deep red from the blood they bathe in. Ignore those with little to live for, as there is no pleasure in their murder. Critical Damage: Target is eviscerated, the spray of their blood restoring STR loss suffered by the Red Cap.
