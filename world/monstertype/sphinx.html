Monster Type

Sphinx

Mythical beasts with the upper body and head of a human, the wings of an eagle, and the body of a lion. Immune to magic of any kind. Poses riddles to anyone they meet. Failure to answer correctly is usually met with death. They know almost everything worth knowing. Roar: Anyone in earshot must save WIL or flee in fear.
