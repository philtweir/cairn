Burrowing Horror

Huge insectoids with multiple legs and shiny black eyes. Subsist from dirt, roots, and flesh. Burrow just beneath surfaces to ambush unsuspecting creatures. Critical Damage: Target loses a body part. Roll 1d6. 1: Head, 2-4: Leg, 5-6: Arm.
